# Customer-Sales-Performance-Analysis  

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends using the Superstore dataset.

The objective is to identify the key drivers of revenue and profit, uncover loss-making areas, evaluate customer profitability, and provide actionable business insights through interactive Tableau dashboards.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

- How are sales and profit performing over time?
- Which regions generate the highest and lowest profit?
- Which categories and sub-categories drive profitability?
- Which products or customers contribute most to profit?
- Are high-sales customers necessarily profitable?
- How does profitability vary across customer segments?
- Which quarters generate the strongest sales and profit?
- Does the highest-sales period also have the highest profit margin?

---

- **Tableau Public** — Data visualization and dashboard development
- **Superstore Dataset** — Sales, customer, product, and regional analysis

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales & Profitability Trends

Monthly analysis shows an overall upward trend in sales, although monthly performance is volatile.

- Highest monthly sales: approximately **$118K**
- Lowest monthly sales: approximately **$2K**
- Highest monthly profit: approximately **$18K**
- Lowest monthly profit: approximately **-$3K**

The analysis demonstrates that increasing sales does not always result in proportional increases in profitability.

---

### 2. Regional Performance

Profit ranking by region:

**West > East > South > Central**

- **West** has the highest total profit.
- **West** also has the highest profit margin.
- **Central** has the lowest total profit.
- **Central** also has the lowest profit margin.

This indicates that regional performance differs not only in sales volume but also in profitability efficiency.

---

### 3. Category & Sub-Category Profitability

**Technology** is the strongest profitability driver.

Major profitable sub-categories include:

- **Copiers** — approximately $56K profit
- **Phones** — approximately $45K profit
- **Accessories** — approximately $42K profit
- **Paper** — approximately $35K profit
- **Binders** — approximately $32K profit

Major loss-making areas include:

- **Tables** — approximately -$18K
- **Bookcases** — approximately -$3.5K
- **Supplies** — slightly negative profit

The analysis highlights the importance of evaluating profitability at the sub-category level rather than relying solely on category-level performance.

---

### 4. Customer Segmentation

| Customer Segment | Customers | Sales | Profit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **High Value** | 198 | $1,139,872 | $253,856 |
| **Profitable Potential** | 90 | $201,431 | $49,536 |
| **High Sales - Low Profit** | 100 | $468,303 | -$37,991 |
| **Low Value** | 412 | $516,929 | $26,895 |

The **High Value** segment is the primary profit driver, generating more than $1.1M in sales and approximately $254K in profit.

A particularly important finding is the **High Sales - Low Profit** segment. These customers generate approximately **$468K in sales but produce a $38K loss**, demonstrating that high revenue does not necessarily mean high profitability.

---

### 5. Top Customers by Profit

The top customer in the High Value segment is **Tamara Chand**, generating approximately **$9K in profit**.

The top 10 customers in this analysis collectively generate approximately **$50K in profit**, highlighting the contribution of high-value customers to overall profitability.

### 6. Quarterly Performance

| Quarter | Sales | Profit | Profit Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Q1** | $366,493 | $49,137 | **13.41%** |
| **Q2** | $450,324 | $55,984 | 12.43% |

This demonstrates that the period generating the highest revenue is not necessarily the period operating at the highest profitability rate.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Revenue does not equal profitability

The High Sales - Low Profit customer segment generated $468K in sales while producing a $38K loss.

### 2. Technology is the strongest profitability driver

Copiers, Phones, and Accessories are among the largest contributors to overall profit.

### 3. Furniture contains significant profitability issues

Tables is the largest loss-making sub-category, followed by Bookcases.

### 4. High Value customers drive the majority of profit

The High Value segment generates approximately $254K in profit from 198 customers.

### 5. Q4 drives the strongest absolute performance

Q4 generates $890K in sales and $113K in profit, making it the strongest quarter by absolute performance.

### 6. Profitability efficiency varies by quarter

Q1 achieves the highest profit margin despite having the lowest quarterly sales and profit.

---

## 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. **Investigate loss-making sub-categories**, particularly Tables and Bookcases, to identify pricing, discount, cost, or product-level issues.

2. **Review High Sales - Low Profit customers** to determine whether excessive discounts, low-margin products, or high fulfillment costs are reducing profitability.

3. **Prioritize High Value customers** through retention strategies and targeted offers.

4. **Expand high-performing Technology products**, particularly Copiers, Phones, and Accessories.

5. **Prepare inventory and marketing strategies ahead of Q4** to capitalize on the strongest sales period.

6. **Monitor Profit Margin alongside Sales** to avoid focusing on revenue growth at the expense of profitability.
